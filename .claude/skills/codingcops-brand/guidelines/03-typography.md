## 3. Typography

**Fonts:** Satoshi (Light 300, Regular 400, Medium 500, Bold 700, Black 900, each with an italic) is the main family. Brittany Signature is the accent script, for about 3% of type. Both files sit in the shared Brand Assets folder.

| Style | Font | Size | Weight | Line height | Tracking | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `display` | Satoshi | 80px | 900 | 1 | -2.4px | Hero headline, Satoshi Black. One per page. |
| `heading` | Satoshi | 56px | 300 | 1.06 | -1.1px | Section titles, Satoshi Light in prussian-blue — the light, airy titles of the brand guide. |
| `heading-sm` | Satoshi | 28px | 700 | 1.1 | -0.5px | Card titles. |
| `subheading` | Satoshi | 24px | 500 | 1.2 | -0.24px | Lead-ins under section titles. |
| `body-lg` | Satoshi | 20px | 400 | 1.4 | 0 | Hero and band supporting copy. |
| `body` | Satoshi | 16px | 400 | 1.5 | 0 | Default running text. |
| `label` | Satoshi | 16px | 700 | 1.2 | 0 | Button and link labels. |
| `body-sm` | Satoshi | 14px | 400 | 1.5 | 0 | Secondary copy, tags, banner text. |
| `caption` | Satoshi | 12px | 500 | 1.5 | 0.12px | Captions and meta in slate. |
| `signature` | Brittany Signature | 56px | 400 | 1.2 | 0 | Accent only, about 3% of type on a page: one or two words laid beside or over a Satoshi headline, a sign-off, a quote flourish. Never body copy, buttons, labels, or anything under 32px. |
| `signature-sm` | Brittany Signature | 36px | 400 | 1.2 | 0 | Smaller accent for cards, social posts and sign-offs. 32px minimum. |
