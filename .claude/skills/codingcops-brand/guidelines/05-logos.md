## 5. Logos

Official CodingCops logo files (transparent PNG). Mark colours: emerald `#00c26c` and `logo-blue` `#004cf8`.

- `codingcops-inline-blue.png`: the default lockup, for `white`, `platinum` and the washes.
- `codingcops-inline-white.png`: the inline lockup with a white wordmark, for `prussian-blue` and photography.
- `codingcops-stack-blue.png` / `codingcops-stack-white.png`: the stacked lockup for centred or square spaces.
- `codingcops-monogram.png`: the mark alone, for favicons, app icons and small badges.

The logo files live in the CodingCops design system and the shared Brand Assets folder. Never redraw or recolour them.
