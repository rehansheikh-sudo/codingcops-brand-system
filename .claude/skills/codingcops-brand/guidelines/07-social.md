## 7. Social

How CodingCops looks on Instagram and LinkedIn. The site is calm and card-led. The feed is louder: blue-washed photos, people cut out like stickers, torn paper and one handwritten line. Build every post from the rules below, and start from the `SocialPost` component.

### Canvas

- Use 1080 × 1350 (4:5) for feed posts and 1080 × 1920 for stories and reels covers. Keep a 64px safe margin on every side.
- The ground is a photo washed in `full-spectrum-blue`. It deepens to `prussian-blue` over the bottom 40% so white type always reads. No flat white posts.

### Photography

- **Backgrounds:** office, team and city shots are pushed to a blue duotone (`prussian-blue` shadows, `full-spectrum-blue` highlights) with light grain or halftone. They are texture, never the subject.
- **People:** cut out at natural colour (greyscale is allowed for a moodier post) with a **white sticker outline** of 6–8px at 1080. Place them in front of the blue ground. This is the feed's version of the system's outlined cards.
- **Emerald blob:** set one large rounded `emerald` shape, echoing the left half of the monogram, behind the subject's shoulders, partly hidden. Use one per post at most and never redraw the full mark.

### Type on posts

- **Headline:** Satoshi Black or Bold, all caps, `white`, 64–88px at 1080, one or two lines ("MEET THE TEAM", "SOFT SKILLS TRAINING").
- **Subline:** Satoshi Medium, sentence or title case, `emerald`, about 32px, with items separated by commas or "|".
- **Torn-paper strip:** for hiring and announcements, use a grey newsprint strip with ripped edges carrying the headline in dark ink ("WE'RE HIRING"). Use at most one strip per post, angled no more than 3°.
- **Script line:** Brittany Signature in `white`, 28–40px at 1080, one or two short lines tilted slightly upward, in a top corner ("learning together today to work better tomorrow"). This is the 3% accent; never use more than one per post.
- **Paper label:** a small white tag with a tape corner, holding a short `vibrant-coral` word ("The Office", "happy birthday!!").

### Signature footer

- Bottom-left: the **monogram** at 56–64px, then a thin white "/", then one script word plus a short caps tagline in Satoshi Medium (for example "upskill BEYOND LIMITS" or "Celebrating OUR STARS").
- For a call to action, add a white outlined pill at bottom-right ("Apply at | name@codingcops.com"). Put the email inside the pill; never use a separate button.

### Series

- **CodingCops Daily** (editorial): a newspaper-masthead treatment, cream newsprint top band, a bold condensed headline and a "Facilitated by" paper label.
- **We're hiring:** cutout subject, torn-paper strip, role title in Satoshi Bold, the requirements in one small line, and the apply pill.
- **Meet the team / culture:** group cutout, caps headline, emerald department subline, monogram only in the footer.
- **Celebrations:** single portrait cutout, emerald blob, coral paper label, "Celebrating OUR STARS" footer.
- Campaigns and events (e.g. an in-office murder mystery) may break the palette with their own theme, but keep the footer lockup.

### Don't

- Don't use stock-style flat illustrations or emoji.
- Don't put white text on `emerald`, and don't put text over a busy, un-washed photo.
- Don't use more than two type styles, plus the script line, on one post.
