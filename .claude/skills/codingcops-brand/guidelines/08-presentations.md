## 8. Presentations

How CodingCops decks look and read. The bar: every slide should make **one point** a person remembers, and the deck should feel like the same brand as the Instagram feed. Start from the `Slide` component.

### Canvas and grid

- Slides are 1920 × 1080 (16:9). Leave 96px margins, use a 12-column grid with 24px gutters, and keep 64px of breathing room above the footer.
- Content slides sit on `platinum`. Title, section and closing slides sit on `prussian-blue`, `full-spectrum-blue` or `emerald`. Rotate those three so each section has its own colour.

### Writing

- **The headline is the takeaway, not the topic.** Write "Attendance climbed 10 points after May", not "Attendance Summary". Keep it to two lines at most and use sentence case.
- Put a small eyebrow above the headline to name the topic ("ATTENDANCE"), in Satoshi Medium caps, 20px, in `full-spectrum-blue` on light slides and `emerald` on dark ones.
- Use at most **3 bullets per slide, 12 words each**. Everything else goes in the speaker notes. Use no square bullets: a short emerald dash, or nothing at all.
- Never paste a raw link. Use a link chip ("HR Drive →") or a QR code.

### Type on slides (at 1920)

| Role | Style |
|---|---|
| Hero number | Satoshi Black, 200–280px, tight tracking |
| Slide headline | Satoshi Bold, 72–88px |
| Section title | Satoshi Black, 140px, plus an outlined section number at 400px |
| Body / bullets | Satoshi Regular, 28–32px; never under 24px |
| Eyebrow and footer | Satoshi Medium caps, 18–20px, letter-spacing 1px |
| Script accent | Brittany Signature, 56–80px, once per section divider or title slide only |

### Shape language

- Cards are the system's cards: `white`, a 2px `prussian-blue` outline (at 1920), `radius-card`, and no shadows. A dark highlight card (`prussian-blue` fill) is allowed **once** per slide, for the number that matters.
- Use an **emerald blob** (one) on title and section slides, bleeding off an edge. Never place it on data slides.
- **People are cutouts with a white sticker outline** over a blue-washed photo, the same as the feed. One hero photo beats a grid of six; when you need several, tilt them ±3° as white-framed polaroids, three at most.

### Data

- Make **one fact per chart**. Colour the bar or line that carries the message `emerald` and everything else `blue-wash`, with `full-spectrum-blue` for the comparison only when needed.
- Label values directly on the bars. Drop gridlines, axis boxes and legends when direct labels work.
- Add one annotation callout per chart (a thin `prussian-blue` line plus a 24px note) saying why the highlighted number matters.
- KPI rows hold **four tiles at most**: a big number, a label in caps and a one-line context in `slate`. Give a change indicator a ▲/▼ arrow plus a word ("up", "down"), not colour alone.

### Logo and footer

- Use the full **inline logo only on the title, section and closing slides**.
- Content slides carry a quiet footer: the monogram (32px), the section name in caps, and the page number at the right, with no blue rule across the top.

### Slide types

1. **Title:** `prussian-blue` ground, eyebrow, a two-line headline (the second line in `full-spectrum-blue` or `emerald`), one script line, an emerald blob and a large outlined period marker ("Q2 '26") on the right.
2. **Section:** a full-bleed colour (rotating), a giant outlined section number, the section title, one script line.
3. **Big number:** one hero number, a takeaway headline and at most 2 lines of context. Use it for the deck's 3–5 most important facts.
4. **KPI + chart:** the takeaway headline, up to 4 KPI tiles on the left, one highlighted chart on the right and one annotation.
5. **Story:** a cutout photo on the left (duotone ground), with the headline plus up to 3 takeaways on the right, for culture, events and CSR.
6. **Closing:** a `prussian-blue` ground, "Thank you" in Satoshi Black with a script flourish, the contact line and the inline white logo.

### Don't

- Don't make bullet walls, six-photo grids, or slides where every card is the same size and colour.
- Don't use coral for anything but small labels and the one warm accent per slide.
- Don't add drop shadows, gradients on cards, or stock icons in colour.
